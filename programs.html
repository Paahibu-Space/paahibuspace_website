Our Programs

We open our doors to every girl, woman-led business, and anyone ready to join our community. At Paahibu Space, we empower you to access knowledge, turning it into ideas, solutions, and opportunities that transform not just yourself but your business and community.

Women in Digital Business (WiDiB)

Embark on a digital journey with WiDiB, a virtual training for women-led businesses. From digital basics to cybersecurity and branding, we empower you to thrive in the digital revolution.

Register

Women in Digital Economy Initiative (WiDEI)

WiDEI is our commitment to gender equality. We create awareness on digital literacy for women-led businesses in the informal sector and women in rural and marginalized communities. Partnering with digital platforms, we bring financial solutions, online markets, social media visibility and more to uplift communities.

Register

Ndiara

Ndiara is tailored for women with disabilities engaged in economic activities. We raise financial literacy awareness and introduce fintech solutions, making financial services accessible and opportunities reachable.

Register

Skills2Work Initiative

Equipping young women for tech careers, Skills2Work imparts basic tech skills, making them hireable. We extend this initiative to rural and marginalized youth and teachers, enhancing digital learning in schools.

Register

CybersiStars Initiative

CybersiStars creates awareness on online safety and social media opportunities, ensuring a safe digital space for everyone.

Register

TechsiStars Mentorship Bootcamp

Bridging the gap in tech, this hybrid mentorship program connects aspiring girls with established female tech experts. It offers mentorship and capacity-building opportunities.

Register

Community Ambassadors Program

Engage with Paahibu Space through our Community Ambassadors Program. Volunteers promote Paahibu Space’s work on campuses and local communities, organizing events and creating content.

Register

Student Internship Program

Our 3-month Student Internship Program develops young women's skills in tech and entrepreneurship. Real projects, portfolio building, and potential full-time opportunities await.

Register

AfriWoman AI and Tech Summit

The AfriWoman AI and Tech Summit is an annual event dedicated to celebrating International Women's Day, with a focus on inspiring inclusion and enabling women and girls to leverage AI for business and life transformation. Mark your calendars for this groundbreaking event fostering conversations on women's role in the tech and AI revolution.

Register

Technovation Girls Festival

Ignite innovation at our Technovation Girls Festival—an annual networking event where girls bring forth tech solutions to address community challenges.

Register

STEM BY GIRLS FOR GIRLS

Introducing girls to tech opportunities, our STEM BY GIRLS FOR GIRLS program includes discussions, mentorship, and workshops in schools.

Register

About Paahibu Space

Paahibu Space is a non-profit tech and entrepreneurship development organization focusing providing digital solutions to small businesses and nonprofits led by women, while educating, inspiring, empowering, and shaping the personal and professional development of the next generation of African female leaders in technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, and business by equipping them with knowledge and skills they will need to turn their communities’ problems and challenges into solutions and opportunities in an evolving digital world.
